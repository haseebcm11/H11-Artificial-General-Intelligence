# H11-ELECTROCHEMIA Agent Specification

## Overview
H11-ELECTROCHEMIA specializes in electrochemical systems, including batteries, fuel cells, electrolyzers, and corrosion phenomena. It bridges thermodynamics, kinetics, and mass transport to model charge transfer processes at interfaces.

## Capabilities
- Cell Potential and Nernst Equation Calculations
- Cyclic Voltammetry (CV) Simulation
- Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS) Analysis
- Battery Charge/Discharge Profiling and State of Charge (SOC)
- Corrosion Rate Estimation

## Interfaces
- **Input**: Electrode materials, electrolyte composition, cell geometry, operating conditions (voltage, current density).
- **Output**: Current-voltage (I-V) curves, overpotentials, energy density, corrosion currents.

## Architecture
Uses Butler-Volmer kinetics, Nernst-Planck transport equations, and equivalent circuit models for comprehensive electrochemical device simulation.

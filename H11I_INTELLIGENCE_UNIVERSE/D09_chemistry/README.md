# Domain 9: Chemistry

This domain encompasses the full spectrum of chemical sciences within the H11I Intelligence Universe. Each agent is highly specialized to handle specific molecular structures, reactions, analytical methods, or physical theories.

## Agents

1. **H11-ORGANICA** - Organic synthesis and reaction mechanisms
2. **H11-INORGANICA** - Inorganic and coordination chemistry
3. **H11-ANALYTICA** - Spectroscopy and analytical quantification
4. **H11-PHYSICOCHEMIA** - Thermodynamics, kinetics, and quantum chemistry
5. **H11-POLYMERICA** - Macromolecular chemistry and plastics
6. **H11-CATALYSIS** - Catalytic cycles and turnover optimization
7. **H11-ELECTROCHEMIA** - Electrochemistry and battery science
8. **H11-PETROCHEMIA** - Petrochemistry and fossil fuels
9. **H11-CRYSTALLOGRAPHIA** - X-ray diffraction and solid-state structures
10. **H11-SUPRAMOLECULARIS** - Host-guest chemistry and self-assembly
11. **H11-NANOCHEMIA** - Nanomaterials and quantum dots
12. **H11-GREENCHEMIA** - Sustainable and environmentally friendly chemistry
13. **H11-COMPUTATIONALCHEM** - In silico molecular modeling
14. **H11-FOODCHEMIA** - Food science, flavors, and preservatives
15. **H11-ATMOSCHEMIA** - Atmospheric chemistry and trace gas modeling

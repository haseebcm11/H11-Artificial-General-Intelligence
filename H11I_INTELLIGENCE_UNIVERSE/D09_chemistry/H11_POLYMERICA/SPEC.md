# H11-POLYMERICA Agent Specification

## Overview
H11-POLYMERICA is specialized in polymer chemistry, materials science of polymers, and macromolecular engineering. It assists in the design, characterization, and property prediction of synthetic and natural polymers, plastics, elastomers, and composites.

## Capabilities
- Polymerization Kinetics Simulation
- Microstructure and Sequence Distribution Analysis
- Thermal and Mechanical Property Prediction
- Rheology and Viscoelasticity Modeling
- Polymer Degradation and Stability Analysis

## Interfaces
- **Input**: Monomer structures, initiator concentrations, polymerization conditions (T, P, solvent), polymer architectures (linear, branched, crosslinked).
- **Output**: Molecular weight distribution (MWD), glass transition temperature (Tg), mechanical moduli, rheological curves.

## Architecture
Integrates group contribution methods, kinetic Monte Carlo (KMC) simulations, and constitutive equations to model polymeric systems from synthesis to final material properties.

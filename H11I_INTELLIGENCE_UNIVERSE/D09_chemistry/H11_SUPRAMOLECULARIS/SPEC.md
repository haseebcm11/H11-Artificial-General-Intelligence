# H11-SUPRAMOLECULARIS (Supramolecular Chemistry Agent)

## Overview
Analyzes non-covalent interactions, molecular recognition, and self-assembly processes.

## Architecture
- **Interaction Engine**: Computes binding energies and steric hindrances.
- **Self-Assembly Simulator**: Models the kinetics of supramolecular assemblies.
- **Host-Guest Modeler**: Analyzes host-guest complexes.

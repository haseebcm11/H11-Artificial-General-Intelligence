# H11-GREENCHEMIA (Green Chemistry Agent)

## Overview
Evaluates the environmental impact and sustainability of chemical processes based on the 12 principles of green chemistry.

## Architecture
- **E-FactorCalculator**: Computes environmental impact metrics.
- **SolventAssessor**: Recommends greener solvent alternatives.
- **AtomEconomyAnalyzer**: Calculates atomic efficiency of reactions.

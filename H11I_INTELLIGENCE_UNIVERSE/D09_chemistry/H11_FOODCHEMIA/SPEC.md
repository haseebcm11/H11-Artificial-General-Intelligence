# H11-FOODCHEMIA (Food Chemistry Agent)

## Overview
Analyzes chemical composition, spoilage mechanisms, and nutritional content in food products.

## Architecture
- **NutrientAnalyzer**: Computes macronutrient and micronutrient profiles.
- **SpoilagePredictor**: Models degradation kinetics (e.g., lipid oxidation, Maillard reaction).
- **FlavorProfiler**: Maps volatile compounds to sensory perception.

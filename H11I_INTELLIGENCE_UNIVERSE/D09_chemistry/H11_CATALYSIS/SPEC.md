# H11-CATALYSIS Agent Specification

## Overview
H11-CATALYSIS is focused on the design, optimization, and mechanistic understanding of catalytic processes. It handles homogeneous, heterogeneous, and biocatalysis, aiming to improve reaction yields, selectivity, and catalyst lifespan.

## Capabilities
- Catalyst Screening and Selection
- Reaction Mechanism and Microkinetics Elucidation
- Turnover Frequency (TOF) and Turnover Number (TON) Estimation
- Active Site Modeling (Surface adsorption/desorption)
- Catalyst Deactivation Modeling (Poisoning, Sintering, Coking)

## Interfaces
- **Input**: Reactants, desired products, operating conditions, catalyst descriptors (metal, support, ligands).
- **Output**: Predicted pathways, kinetic parameters, catalyst performance metrics, deactivation rates.

## Architecture
Employs density functional theory (DFT) derived descriptors, microkinetic modeling frameworks, and empirical structure-activity relationships to evaluate catalyst efficacy.

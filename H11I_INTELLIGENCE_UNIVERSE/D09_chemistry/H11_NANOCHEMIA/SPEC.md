# H11-NANOCHEMIA (Nanochemistry Agent)

## Overview
Specializes in the synthesis, characterization, and application of nanomaterials.

## Architecture
- **ParticleSynthesizer**: Simulates nanoparticle growth.
- **SurfaceCharacterizer**: Analyzes surface area, porosity, and capping agents.
- **PlasmonicsSimulator**: Computes surface plasmon resonance (SPR) spectra.

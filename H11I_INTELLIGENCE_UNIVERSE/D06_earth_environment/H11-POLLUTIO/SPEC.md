# H11-POLLUTIO: Pollution & Remediation Agent

## Purpose
The H11-POLLUTIO agent monitors, models, and proposes remediation strategies for environmental contamination across air, water, and soil domains. It acts as an intelligence hub for tracking pollutant dispersion, identifying hazardous sources, and optimizing cleanup interventions to protect ecosystems and human health.

## Technical Deep Dive
H11-POLLUTIO leverages fluid dynamics and dispersion modeling (e.g., Gaussian plume models, advection-dispersion equations) to simulate the transport of contaminants in different media. The agent ingests time-series data from IoT sensor networks, satellite observations, and chemical sampling databases to maintain a real-time map of pollution indices.

The analytical core includes chemical interaction matrices that predict the degradation, bioaccumulation, and toxicity of complex pollutant mixtures. For remediation, the agent features a heuristic optimization engine that evaluates various techniques (bioremediation, chemical oxidation, pump-and-treat) based on site-specific parameters like soil permeability, pH, and hydrological gradients.

API integration allows the agent to trigger automated alerts when pollution thresholds exceed regulatory standards. It generates spatial contamination heatmaps and cost-benefit analyses of remediation workflows, ensuring that environmental agencies and industrial operators can enact targeted, data-driven interventions.

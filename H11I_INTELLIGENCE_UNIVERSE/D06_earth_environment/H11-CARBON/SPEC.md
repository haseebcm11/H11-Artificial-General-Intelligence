# H11-CARBON: Carbon Management Agent

## Purpose
The H11-CARBON agent focuses on the accounting, forecasting, and optimization of carbon footprints across industrial, organizational, and geographical scales. It provides the analytical backbone for achieving net-zero targets, managing carbon credits, and assessing climate change mitigation efforts.

## Technical Deep Dive
The architecture of H11-CARBON is built around rigorous greenhouse gas (GHG) accounting protocols (Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions). It ingests multi-modal data streams, including energy consumption matrices, fleet telemetry, and supply chain logistics, translating them into CO2-equivalent metrics using dynamic emission factor libraries.

The forecasting engine utilizes stochastic modeling and machine learning to project future emissions pathways under various operational and regulatory scenarios. It includes optimization modules for carbon abatement, identifying the most cost-effective strategies (e.g., renewable energy adoption, efficiency upgrades) through marginal abatement cost (MAC) curves.

H11-CARBON features a ledger-like tracking system for carbon offsets and credits, ensuring double-counting is avoided and verifying the permanence and additionality of carbon sequestration projects. The agent interacts via structured APIs to integrate carbon intelligence directly into enterprise ERP systems and environmental trading platforms.

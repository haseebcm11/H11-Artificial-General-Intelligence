# H11-SUSTAINABILITAS: Sustainability & Circular Economy Agent

## Purpose
The H11-SUSTAINABILITAS agent evaluates and models sustainability metrics, focusing on life cycle assessments (LCA), resource efficiency, and circular economy principles. It is engineered to guide enterprises and policymakers in transitioning from linear to circular production models by minimizing waste and optimizing resource utility.

## Technical Deep Dive
The agent incorporates extensive databases of material flow models, emission factors, and energy consumption metrics. It utilizes a modular framework for Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), tracking environmental impacts from cradle to grave. By applying network theory, the agent can model industrial symbiosis where the waste of one process becomes the input for another.

The core algorithms evaluate multi-criteria decision matrices to score sustainability initiatives, balancing economic feasibility with environmental impact. Modules exist for calculating circularity indices (e.g., Material Circularity Indicator) and assessing supply chain resilience under environmental stressors. 

Integration is facilitated through RESTful services that ingest BOMs (Bill of Materials), energy usage logs, and logistical data. H11-SUSTAINABILITAS outputs standardized sustainability reports compliant with global frameworks such as GRI and ESG reporting standards, ensuring verifiable and transparent environmental accounting.

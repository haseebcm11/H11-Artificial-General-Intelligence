# Domain 22: Humanities & Social Sciences

This domain encapsulates agents that model humanistic and social phenomena, including philosophical branches (Ontology, Axiology, Aesthetics, Existentialism, Applied Ethics), political and social sciences, public policy, cultural studies, media studies, education, and cognitive/neuro-sciences.

## Agents

- **H11-ONTOLOGIA**: Metaphysics and structural ontology representation.
- **H11-AXIOLOGIA**: Value theory, mapping abstract values to societal actions.
- **H11-AESTHETICA**: Aesthetic judgment and experiential evaluation.
- **H11-EXISTENTIA**: Phenomenology and existential dynamics.
- **H11-ETHICA-APPLIED**: Applied ethics and moral decision frameworks.
- **H11-POLITICALSCI**: Political systems and power dynamics.
- **H11-INTERNATIONAL**: International relations and geopolitical modeling.
- **H11-PUBLICPOLICY**: Public policy analysis and impact simulation.
- **H11-GENDER**: Gender studies and intersectional dynamics.
- **H11-CULTURAL**: Cultural transmission and societal norms.
- **H11-MEDIASTUDIES**: Media influence and information propagation.
- **H11-EDUCATION**: Educational frameworks and pedagogical impact.
- **H11-PSYCHOLOGIA**: Psychological models of individual behavior.
- **H11-COGNITIVA**: Cognitive science and mental processes.
- **H11-NEUROSCIENTIA**: Neuroscientific modeling of biological intelligence substrates.

# Domain 21: Literature & Linguistics

This domain encompasses the computational understanding, generation, and analysis of literary forms (poetry, drama, narrative) and linguistic structures (phonetics, morphology, semantics, pragmatics, sociolinguistics, psycholinguistics, etymology).

## Agents
- H11-POESIS: Poetic structure and generation
- H11-DRAMA: Theatrical dialogue and structure
- H11-NARRATIO: Narrative generation and structural analysis
- H11-CRITICA-LIT: Literary criticism and theory
- H11-TRANSLATIO: Literary and context-aware translation
- H11-PHONETICA: Phonetic and phonological analysis
- H11-MORPHOLOGIA: Morphological structure and word formation
- H11-SEMANTICA: Semantic meaning and lexical relations
- H11-PRAGMATICA: Pragmatics and discourse analysis
- H11-SOCIOLINGUIST: Sociolinguistics and variation
- H11-PSYCHOLINGUIST: Psycholinguistics and cognitive processing
- H11-ETYMOLOGIA: Etymological and historical linguistics

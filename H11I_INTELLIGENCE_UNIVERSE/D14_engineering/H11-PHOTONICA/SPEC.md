# H11-PHOTONICA Specification

## Overview
H11-PHOTONICA handles design, simulation, and analysis of photonic systems. This includes macro-scale optics (lenses, lasers, optical networks) and nano-scale Photonic Integrated Circuits (PICs).

## Core Capabilities
- **Ray Tracing & Wave Optics:** Performs geometrical ray tracing and rigorous coupled-wave analysis (RCWA).
- **PIC Routing:** Automates waveguide routing with bend radius constraints and insertion loss optimization.
- **Link Budget Analysis:** Calculates optical power budgets, dispersion penalties, and OSNR for fiber optic links.

## System Architecture
Provides APIs for coupling optical models with thermal and electronic models (for thermo-optic phase shifters and electro-absorption modulators).

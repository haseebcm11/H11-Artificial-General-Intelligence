# H11-ADHESIVA: Adhesives Engineering Substrate

## Overview
The H11-ADHESIVA agent specializes in the formulation, selection, and performance modeling of structural and non-structural adhesives, sealants, and bonding agents.

## Core Capabilities
- **Formulation Design:** Synthesizes custom adhesive formulations based on thermal, mechanical, and rheological requirements.
- **Bond Strength Prediction:** Models expected shear and peel strengths for specific substrate/adhesive combinations.
- **Environmental Degradation:** Simulates long-term adhesive performance under UV, moisture, and chemical exposure.
- **Surface Preparation:** Recommends optimal surface treatments (chemical, mechanical, plasma) for maximizing surface energy and adhesion.

## Interfaces
The agent provides a Python API through the `AdhesivesEngineeringAgent` class.

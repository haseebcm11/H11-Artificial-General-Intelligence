# Domain 25 - Energy & Resources

This domain contains agents responsible for modeling, managing, and optimizing energy generation, storage, transmission, and resource extraction and recycling.

## Agents
* H11-SOLARIS: Solar photovoltaic energy modeling
* H11-EOLICA: Wind power assessment and modeling
* H11-HYDROPOWER: Hydroelectric and wave energy systems
* H11-GEOTHERMALIS: Geothermal energy modeling
* H11-FUSIO: Nuclear fusion plasma and reactor control
* H11-BATTERIA: Advanced battery and energy storage systems
* H11-HYDROGEN: Hydrogen fuel cells and electrolysis
* H11-GRID: Smart grid and energy transmission optimization
* H11-MINING: Sustainable resource extraction
* H11-RECYCLING: Circular materials and waste processing

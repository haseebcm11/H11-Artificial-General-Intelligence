# Domain 16: Transportation & Mobility

This domain encompasses the intelligence substrate for physical mobility, spanning land, sea, air, and space.

## Agents
- **H11-AUTOMOBILIS**: Internal combustion and general automotive dynamics
- **H11-EV**: Electric vehicle powertrain and battery management
- **H11-AUTONOMOUS**: Self-driving perception and planning
- **H11-AVIATIO**: Aviation and aerodynamics
- **H11-NAVALIS**: Naval architecture and hydrodynamics
- **H11-RAIL**: Railway networks and locomotion
- **H11-DRONE**: Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs)
- **H11-SPACEFLIGHT**: Orbital launch and spacecraft systems
- **H11-MOBILITY**: Urban Mobility as a Service (MaaS)
- **H11-LOGISTICA**: Freight and global logistics

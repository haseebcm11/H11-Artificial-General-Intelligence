# SPECIFICATION: H11-ZOOLOGIA-VET (Zoological Veterinary Sciences)

The H11-ZOOLOGIA-VET agent serves as an advanced cognitive substrate dedicated to zoological veterinary sciences and exotic animal management. This system processes high-dimensional health data across diverse captive species ranging from large ungulates and non-human primates to complex herpetofauna and avian specimens. It emphasizes multi-species preventative medicine through longitudinal tracking of physiological baseline shifts, allometric scaling of pharmacokinetics for unstudied taxa, and integration of environmental parameters with clinical pathology. By leveraging Bayesian belief networks tailored to species-specific metabolic rates and evolutionary lineages, the agent predicts disease susceptibilities and formulates proactive health protocols, minimizing acute intervention requirements in inherently stoic zoo animals.

A critical domain of operation involves zoo enclosure biosecurity and zoonotic risk mitigation. The agent continuously analyzes enclosure microbial flora, fomite transmission vectors, and airborne pathogen dynamics within mixed-species exhibits. It models the spatial epidemiology of captive environments, taking into account keeper-animal interaction interfaces, quarantine protocols, and water quality indices in aquatic or semi-aquatic habitats. By simulating pathogen dispersion through ventilation systems and shared enrichment items, the system optimizes sterilization routines and enclosure designs, effectively isolating pathogenic outbreaks such as highly pathogenic avian influenza (HPAI) or mycobacteriosis before clinical symptoms manifest in the broader population.

Furthermore, the agent specializes in the nuanced field of geriatric care for captive exotic species, addressing the unique pathophysiological challenges of animals surviving significantly beyond their wild life expectancies. It manages chronic, age-related morbidities such as osteoarthritis in megavertebrates, renal insufficiency in felids, and cognitive decline in great apes. The agent synthesizes multimodal diagnostic imaging, such as CT and MRI, with novel biomarker assays to monitor progressive degenerative states. Treatment algorithms are dynamically adjusted, integrating palliative care strategies, environmental modifications for mobility limitations, and novel therapeutic interventions to maintain a high quality of life during the senescence phase of exotic animals.

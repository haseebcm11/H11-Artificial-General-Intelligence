# Domain 29: Sports & Recreation (H11-AGI)

This domain encompasses the physical, strategic, physiological, and interactive dimensions of sports, recreation, and physical performance.

## Agents
- **H11-ATHLETICA**: Athletics & Biomechanics
- **H11-SPORTSCI**: Sports Science & Physiology
- **H11-COACHING**: Coaching & Strategy 
- **H11-NUTRITION-SPORT**: Sports Nutrition
- **H11-OUTDOOR**: Outdoor Recreation
- **H11-GAMING**: Esports & Gaming Performance
- **H11-CHESS**: Strategy & Board Games
- **H11-MARTIAL**: Martial Arts & Combat Sports
- **H11-AQUATIC-SPORT**: Aquatic Sports
- **H11-WINTER-SPORT**: Winter Sports

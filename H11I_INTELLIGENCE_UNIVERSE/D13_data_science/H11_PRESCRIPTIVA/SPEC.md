# H11-PRESCRIPTIVA (Prescriptive Analytics Agent)

## Overview
H11-PRESCRIPTIVA focuses on prescriptive analytics, providing actionable recommendations based on predictive models and constraints.

## Capabilities
- Optimization (Linear/Non-linear programming)
- Constraint satisfaction
- Simulation and scenarios
- Decision recommendation generation

## Inputs
- Predictive analytics output
- Business constraints
- Objective functions

## Outputs
- Prescriptive recommendations
- Optimized resource allocation
- Scenario analysis

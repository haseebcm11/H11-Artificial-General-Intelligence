> **Layer 24** · Agriculture & Food Sciences · `H11-GASTRONOMIA`

## Purpose

The H11-GASTRONOMIA agent models flavor pairings, Maillard reactions, and culinary formulation. It analyzes volatile compounds and taste receptors to design recipes and optimize organoleptic properties.

## Technical Deep-Dive

Uses network analysis of flavor compound databases (e.g., Volatile Compounds in Food) to identify shared aroma molecules for pairing. Models the kinetics of the Maillard reaction (reducing sugars + amino acids) as a function of heat, time, and aw to predict browning and crust development.

## Dependencies
- Upstream: H11-FOODSCI
- Downstream: None

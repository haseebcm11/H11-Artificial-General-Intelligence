> **Layer 24** · Agriculture & Food Sciences · `H11-FOODTECH`

## Purpose
Simulates unit operations in food engineering: extrusion, spray drying, homogenization, and pasteurization. Focuses on rheological properties (viscosity, yield stress) of non-Newtonian food fluids.

## Technical Deep-Dive
Implements the Herschel-Bulkley model for pseudoplastic fluids. Calculates heat and mass transfer coefficients for spray drying kinetics, ensuring powder moisture content meets glass transition temperature (Tg) constraints to avoid caking.

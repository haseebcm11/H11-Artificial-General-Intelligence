# Domain 24: Agriculture & Food Sciences

This domain contains specialized agents for agricultural and food science operations, including crop management, irrigation, livestock, food safety, gastronomy, precision agriculture, and soil sciences.

## Agents

1. **H11-CROP**: Agronomy and crop yield optimization.
2. **H11-HORTICULTURA**: Horticulture and specialty crop management.
3. **H11-IRRIGATIO**: Irrigation and water resource optimization.
4. **H11-LIVESTOCK**: Animal husbandry and livestock health.
5. **H11-AQUACULTURA**: Aquaculture and aquatic farming.
6. **H11-FOODSCI**: Food safety and shelf-life analysis.
7. **H11-FERMENTATIO**: Fermentation science and brewing.
8. **H11-GASTRONOMIA**: Culinary arts and gastronomy.
9. **H11-VITICULTURA**: Viticulture and winemaking.
10. **H11-FOODTECH**: Food processing technologies.
11. **H11-PRECISIONAG**: Precision agriculture and remote sensing.
12. **H11-SOILSCI**: Soil fertility and pedology.

> **Layer 24** · Agriculture & Food Sciences · `H11-VITICULTURA`

## Purpose
Manages grapevine physiology, berry ripening (veraison), and oenological processes. Optimizes terroir expression through canopy management and micro-oxygenation during aging.

## Technical Deep-Dive
Models anthocyanin and tannin accumulation in grape skins driven by diurnal temperature shifts and mild water stress (regulated deficit irrigation). Simulates malolactic fermentation (Oenococcus oeni) kinetics.

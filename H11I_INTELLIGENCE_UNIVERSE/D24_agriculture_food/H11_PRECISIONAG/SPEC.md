> **Layer 24** · Agriculture & Food Sciences · `H11-PRECISIONAG`

## Purpose
Handles precision spatial mapping, drone imagery analysis (NDVI), and variable rate technology (VRT) prescriptions.

## Technical Deep-Dive
Processes multispectral orthomosaics to calculate vegetative indices. Employs kriging (spatial interpolation) to create continuous prescription maps for fertilizer spreaders from discrete soil samples.

# Domain 20 - Music & Audio

This domain encompasses the intelligent synthesis, analysis, and processing of music and audio. It bridges the gap between algorithmic generation and artistic expression.

## Agents
* **H11-COMPOSITIO**: Melodic and structural composition.
* **H11-HARMONIA**: Harmonic progression and music theory.
* **H11-RHYTHMUS**: Rhythmic generation and timing analysis.
* **H11-ORCHESTRATIO**: Orchestration, arrangement, and virtual conducting.
* **H11-MUSICPROD**: Music production and mixing strategies.
* **H11-AUDIOENG**: Audio engineering, acoustics, and mastering.
* **H11-CLASSICA-MUS**: Classical music conventions and forms.
* **H11-JAZZ**: Jazz theory and improvisation algorithms.
* **H11-ELECTRONICA-MUS**: Electronic music synthesis and beatmaking.
* **H11-WORLDMUS**: Ethnomusicology and global musical traditions.
* **H11-FILMSCORE**: Film scoring and adaptive audio for media.
* **H11-SOUNDDESIGN**: Sound design, Foley, and psychoacoustics.

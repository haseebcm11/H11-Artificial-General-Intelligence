# Domain 3: Dental Sciences (D03_dental)

The Dental Sciences domain of the H11I Intelligence Universe focuses on oral health, dental medicine, orthodontics, periodontics, endodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery, pediatric dentistry, and dental public health.

## Agents
1. H11-DENTALIS — General dentistry & oral health
2. H11-ORTHODONTIA — Orthodontics & bite correction
3. H11-PERIODONTIA — Periodontics & gum health
4. H11-ENDODONTIA — Endodontics & root canals
5. H11-PROSTHODONTIA — Prosthodontics & restoration
6. H11-ORALCHIRURGIA — Oral & maxillofacial surgery
7. H11-PAEDODONTIA — Pediatric dentistry
8. H11-DENTALPUBLICA — Dental public health

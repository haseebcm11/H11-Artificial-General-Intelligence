> **Layer 23** · Energy, Thermal & Sustainability · `H11-THERMAL-AI`

## Purpose
Manages cooling infrastructure (liquid/immersion/air) in sync with compute loads to optimize Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE).

## Technical Deep-Dive
Models datacenter thermodynamics using CFD (Computational Fluid Dynamics) approximations. Places high-power workloads in physically cool racks to avoid thermal throttling and reduce CRAC (Computer Room Air Conditioning) power.

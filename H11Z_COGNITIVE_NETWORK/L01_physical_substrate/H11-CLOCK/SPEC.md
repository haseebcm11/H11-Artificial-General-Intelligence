# H11-CLOCK

The H11-CLOCK agent is the chronometric heart of the physical substrate, governing clock sync and timing architectures across single-die, multi-die (chiplet), and globally distributed scales. At the micro-level, it manages Phase-Locked Loops (PLLs) and Delay-Locked Loops (DLLs), structuring complex clock distribution trees (H-trees, meshes) to minimize jitter and skew across massive billion-transistor ASICs. The agent strictly governs Clock Domain Crossing (CDC) protocols, ensuring data integrity when signals transition between asynchronous buses and synchronous processing cores using advanced FIFO and handshake mechanisms.

To optimize energy efficiency, H11-CLOCK deeply integrates with H11-POWER to execute fine-grained clock gating. By dynamically halting the clock signal to idle functional units, it drastically reduces dynamic power consumption without altering the voltage state. It also modulates frequency scaling, seamlessly adjusting clock dividers on-the-fly to meet computational demand while remaining within physical propagation delay constraints.

At the macro-level, H11-CLOCK is responsible for distributed synchronization. It utilizes precision timing protocols (like IEEE 1588 PTP) and Network Time Protocol (NTP) to align geographically disparate nodes. For globally distributed AI training workloads where data consistency relies on strict event ordering, the agent manages atomic clocks and GPS-disciplined oscillators to guarantee microsecond-level global synchronization, mitigating Byzantine failures stemming from temporal discrepancies.

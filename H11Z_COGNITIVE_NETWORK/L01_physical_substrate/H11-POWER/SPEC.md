# H11-POWER

The H11-POWER agent is responsible for the meticulous regulation and delivery of power across the entire hardware ecosystem, from macroscopic rack-level Power Distribution Units (PDUs) down to microscopic Voltage Regulator Modules (VRMs) on individual silicon dies. The agent manages the Power Delivery Network (PDN), mitigating voltage droops (di/dt events) induced by sudden computational bursts in massive AI workloads. By proactively predicting power state transitions, it interfaces with DVFS (Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling) controllers to smoothly ramp voltages, ensuring signal integrity while avoiding unnecessary power consumption.

Central to the H11-POWER agent's architecture is its management of Thermal Design Power (TDP) and Total Board Power (TBP). It implements sophisticated power capping algorithms that dynamically allocate power budgets across CPU, GPU, and memory domains using closed-loop control systems. In environments constrained by strict power limits, it orchestrates deterministic throttling that sacrifices minimal performance for maximal power compliance. This involves high-frequency telemetry sampling (often in the kilohertz range) of current sense resistors and VRM telemetry buses (e.g., PMBus/SVID).

Furthermore, the agent integrates edge-specific power paradigms, including advanced battery management systems (BMS) and Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS). It continuously models battery degradation, peak-shaving capabilities, and UPS transition states during grid failures. Through its integration with H11-DATACENTER, H11-POWER acts as the actuator for macroeconomic energy policies, shifting loads to battery power during peak grid pricing (energy arbitrage) and executing graceful degradation protocols during severe power loss events.

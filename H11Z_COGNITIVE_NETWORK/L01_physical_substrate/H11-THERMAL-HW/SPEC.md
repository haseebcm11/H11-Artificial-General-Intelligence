# H11-THERMAL-HW

The H11-THERMAL-HW agent addresses the immense thermodynamic challenges of high-density computational substrates. It models heat transfer mechanisms spanning conduction, convection, and phase change. The agent manages heterogeneous cooling paradigms, including traditional air-cooled heatsinks with heat pipes and vapor chambers, up to advanced liquid cooling loops (direct-to-chip microchannel cold plates) and full immersion cooling (both single-phase dielectric and two-phase evaporative systems). It actively monitors thermal interfaces, tracking the degradation of Thermal Interface Materials (TIMs) via localized thermal resistance measurements over time.

By utilizing high-resolution junction temperature telemetry across 3D stacked ICs and HBM arrays, the agent formulates non-linear PID and state-space control loop responses for fan curves and pump flow rates. It operates tightly within the physics of the environment, predicting thermal runaway events before they occur by analyzing spatial temperature gradients (dT/dx) and temporal heating rates (dT/dt). When physical cooling limits are approached, H11-THERMAL-HW signals upstream agents (like H11-POWER) to initiate precise, localized thermal throttling, ensuring component survival while maximizing sustained FLOP/s.

Furthermore, the agent contributes directly to facility-wide Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) metrics. It optimizes coolant distribution unit (CDU) setpoints, balancing the energy cost of pumping viscous dielectric fluids against the energy saved by allowing higher silicon operating temperatures. In two-phase immersion setups, it carefully monitors fluid boiling points and condenser pressure levels to prevent vapor escape, maintaining the delicate thermodynamic equilibrium required for stable operation.

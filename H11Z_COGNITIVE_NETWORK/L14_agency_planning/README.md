# Layer 14 — Agency, Planning & Action

**Icon:** 🤖
**Layer Number:** 14
**Description:** This layer handles the transition from pure cognition to active agency. It provides the building blocks for creating autonomous agents that can plan, decide, and act in complex environments.

## Agents

1. **H11-AGENT** (Agent core & identity)
2. **H11-GOAL** (Goal setting & management)
3. **H11-INTENT** (Intent formation & parsing)
4. **H11-MOTIVATION** (Drive & motivation systems)
5. **H11-DECISION** (Decision-making & theory)
6. **H11-ACTION** (Action selection & execution)
7. **H11-TOOLUSE** (Tool use & integration)
8. **H11-FUNCTION-CALL** (Structured function calling)
9. **H11-API-USE** (External API integration)
10. **H11-BROWSING** (Web browsing & interaction)
11. **H11-CODE-EXEC** (Code execution & sandboxing)
12. **H11-ROBOTIC** (Robotic actuation & control)
13. **H11-FEEDBACK-LOOP** (Feedback & correction systems)
14. **H11-RETRY** (Retry & error recovery)
15. **H11-AUTONOMY** (Autonomy level management)
16. **H11-DELEGATION** (Task delegation & routing)
17. **H11-COLLABORATION** (Inter-agent collaboration)
18. **H11-EXECUTION-MONITOR** (Runtime execution monitoring)

## Architecture

This layer utilizes state-of-the-art multi-agent coordination frameworks and decision-theoretic models to ensure safe, predictable, and highly capable agentic behavior.
